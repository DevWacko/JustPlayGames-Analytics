# T&T Regulations Test Trainer

A static website for practising the Trinidad and Tobago driver's regulations
(written) test. Open `index.html` in any browser; no build step or server needed.

## What it covers

Content comes from the TTRegs.com printable study guides (printed 15 Nov 2026):

- **Private car & motorcycle (Class 1 & 3):** 85 multiple choice questions
- **Heavy & extra heavy vehicles (Class 4 & 5):** 62 multiple choice questions
- **Taxi (public service vehicle):** 62 multiple choice questions
- **65 road signs** (danger warning, regulatory, informative) and **6 hand signals**, shared by all three

## Features

- Pick your test type, then practise by category or study the answers first.
- Each answer is marked right or wrong straight away, with the correct one shown.
- Mock test: 14 multiple choice, 12 road signs and 4 hand signals.
- Results show a pass/fail verdict against an adjustable pass mark (80% by default,
  an estimate; the Licensing Division does not publish the real figure online).
- "Review my mistakes" and per-category best scores, saved in the browser.

## Files

- `index.html` – the page, styles and quiz logic
- `data.js` – questions, answers, sign images (embedded) and hand signals

The study guide content belongs to TTRegs.com and is included for personal study.
